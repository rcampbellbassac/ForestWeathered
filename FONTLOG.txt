FONTLOG for Forest Weathered

Forest Weathered (ໝາກກໍ່) is a display font for Lao, Thai, Khmer and Latin drawn from a hand-painted
sign photographed in Laos in 2019. Outdoor rustic brush lettering on green-painted wood: sweeping rounded contours, spurred terminals. Weathering is not built into the outlines (heavier smoothing fills the paint loss); a distressed version can be a separate style later.

How it was made
- Lao key letters (ໄ ມ ້ ກ) were traced from the photograph (scripts/segment.py, scripts/trace.py).
- The other letters were generated on the Noto designs with a simulated brush whose size and the font's width were
  measured from the traced letters (scripts/auto.py), then merged with the traced letters (scripts/hybrid.py).
- Generated letters are drafts to be redrawn toward the traced ones before 1.0.
